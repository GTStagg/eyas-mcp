# Support

eyas-mcp is a personal project with no support commitment or service level. Please don't contact CrowdStrike about it: they don't maintain or support this project.

Questions, bugs, ideas and feedback go in this repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GTStagg/eyas-mcp/issues/new/choose). Ideas and feedback are welcome; pull requests from outside the project aren't accepted (see [CONTRIBUTING](.github/CONTRIBUTING.md)). To report a vulnerability, follow the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md).
